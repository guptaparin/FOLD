# FOLD — concept and storyboard

## Directions considered
1. **One sheet** — a single square of paper carries the whole film. It folds, comes alive,
   unfolds and refolds into the next creature, and finally folds into the end card. Pure
   tactile ASMR: macro paper, close sound. *Chosen:* it is the product's own loop, shown
   at its most satisfying, and the carrier object never changes.
2. **Night lights** — a dark paper world relit lantern by lantern as each chapter is solved.
   Beautiful, but the folding (the hands-first proof) becomes secondary.
3. **Desk diary** — a day at the desk from first light to moonrise, one fold per hour. Strong
   arc, but too slow for a 32 s piece and weak in the first second.

## Signature moments
- The first crease: a fingertip presses a fold flat in macro and the room *hears* it.
- The flurry: a sheet folds itself into a swan in seven quick snaps, one per half-beat.
- The release: every lantern on the lake lifts into the night at once.
