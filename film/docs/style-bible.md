# FOLD film — style bible

## Palette (6)
| Token | Hex | Use |
|---|---|---|
| Dusk navy | `#0b1024` | Night sky, deep shadow, vignette |
| Ember | `#e2704f` / `#d9603f` | The first sheet, the URL pill, warmth |
| Washi cream | `#f4ead6` | Paper backs, end card |
| Lantern gold | `#ffc488` | Key light, sparks, lanterns |
| Moss mat | `#23463c` | Cutting mat, the stage the folds happen on |
| Ink | `#2b2118` | End-card type |

All paper patterns come from the game's own generator (seigaiha, asanoha, kikko, dots, stripes, sakura).

## Type
- **Fraunces Variable** (local, `@fontsource-variable/fraunces`): every line on screen; italic for the voice of the hand ("with your hands.").
- **Inter Variable** (local): the platform line and the URL only.
- Type enters word by word, unmasking left to right like a crease running across, with a 26 px rise;
  it exits fast with blur. Each line holds ≥ 1.8 s. A soft radial plate sits behind every line.

## Light and lens
- Warm key from the desk lantern's side (`#ffc488`), cool moon from above-right, warm fill from camera.
- Macro and diorama shots use miniature focus: blur bands at the top and bottom of frame (tilt-shift).
- Camera always drifts a few millimetres; nothing ever freezes.

## Motion vocabulary
- **Fold:** ease-in-out around the real crease hinge; the flap's speed peaks mid-arc.
- **Crease:** lands on the beat with a glow pulse and sparks along the hinge.
- **Turn-over:** the sheet lifts 7 cm and flips about its centre line; the next sheet is revealed underneath.
- **Life:** creases relax open, the model lifts on an arc and lands with a settle.
- **Exit:** fast, with blur; entrances slow enough to read.

## Sound vocabulary
- Paper: sparse crackle grains following the fold speed, low sheet-bend body, crisp crease snap
  (transient + nail "zip" + mat thump), flutter whoosh for turn-overs.
- Music: D major pentatonic koto (Karplus–Strong), warm pad (I–vi–IV–V), soft felt pulse on downbeats, room air.
- Every crease earns a koto note that climbs the scale.
